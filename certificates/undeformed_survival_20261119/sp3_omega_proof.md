For each trace length, enumerate exactly the components of

and their total θ and holomorphic derivatives. On the Cartan this
is exhaustively modeled as follows:

1. A base component picks a (multi)set of L slots of the bosonic
   θ derivative coefficients φ1,φ2,φ3 and the odd coordinate
   derivative coefficients λ+,λ− (each odd direction at most once

   trace of any fixed ordered product, up to a nonzero integer
   multinomial factor. All such integers have prime factors at
   most L≤10. Trace rank is six, so odd L has zero Cartan image.
2. Use each total θ derivative at most once per factor and total
   z derivatives up to the required n+,n− per direction. All charges
   of a product are positive; the excess degree relative to L
   is just three in this window and all its Weyl extraction offsets.
   Thus this finite grammar spans the Cartan images of all
   multi-gravitons. Cartan factor recipes and signs were check-matched
   against actual literal Sp6 trace cochains at L=2,4.
3. At each charge, one-color polynomial spans of each single-trace
   factor are *exactly* reduced to an independent mod-p basis. The
   full factor repeats the same polynomial at each of three colors,
   so this retains exactly its subspace. Products are multilinear,
   hence products of these reduced factor bases still exhaust the image.
4. At the end of each product, keeping one monomial coefficient per
   S3 color-permutation orbit is an injective storage projection:
   the sum is color invariant, with signs determined by odd reordering.
   Product cochain span ranks are then exact modular polynomial ranks,
   not ranks guessed from random fixtures. A bounded interrupted run
   certifies only its completed log ranks; use the completed JSON

5. The family span is GL(3)×GL(2)-stable (these are complete
   components and derivatives, then all products). At p=1000003
   much larger than the total polynomial degrees 11 and 2,
   this polynomial representation is semisimple. Therefore the Weyl
   signed weight extraction on exact modular ranks is legitimate.
   This is not a subtraction of sampled rank lower bounds. A
   p-local highest-weight projector (denominators nonzero for the
   small Casimir eigenvalues) lifts the resulting modular HW basis
   to actual QQ HW combinations with at least this Cartan rank.
   Q0 boundaries have identically zero Cartan image, so these are
   genuinely independent QQ H(Q0) classes.
