# Discriminant dressing of the full R-007 perturbative class

In the homogeneous relative jet complex of undeformed four-dimensional $SU(3)$ Yang–Mills theory with $\mathcal N=4$, let $F$ be any homogeneous perturbative completion of the 6,139-term R-007 representative. Let $\Delta$ be the discriminant of $\phi^1(0)$ and let $\star$ be the marked quantum product. Then

$$
[\Delta]^{70}\star[F]
\quad\text{belongs to the perturbative subalgebra generated by completed single gravitons.}
$$

Powers and products in this statement are taken in cohomology. The sufficient exponent 70 is independent of the chosen homogeneous corrections. Dot supplied characteristic-zero existence and absence of coupling torsion for R-007. Kibe, van Leuven and Mathieson supplied the classical localization argument. The first exponent 6 below is the existing polynomial R-007 primitive bound. ## A classical clearing bound

Put $A=\phi^1(0)$. Kibe–van Leuven–Mathieson's weight is $W=n_++n_-+\theta_2+\theta_3$. Every relative jet except $A$ has positive $W$. At $W>0$ a polynomial classical cycle $X$ admits a polynomial graviton expression $G$ and a polynomial invariant $H$ satisfying

$$
\Delta^{2W}X=G+Q_0H .
$$

On the diagonal chart write $z_i$ for the three distinct eigenvalues of $A$. The inverse of the three-by-three Vandermonde matrix has in its $i$th row the coefficients of the polynomial $\prod_{j\ne i}(t-z_j)/(z_i-z_j)$. Each entry has pole order at most one at each individual eigenvalue gap. At weight $w$, the graviton with linear Cartan term $\operatorname{Tr}(A^{d-1}Y)$ also has terms containing at least two positive-weight letters, of smaller weights that add to $w$. Here $Y$ has weight $w$, and the two graviton rows have $d=2,3$. Tracelessness supplies the remaining row. The recursion for a closed diagonal replacement in equations (105)–(107) gives a gap pole of order at most $2w-1$. Indeed, with $k\ge2$ children of weights $w_i$, one inverse Vandermonde adds at most one to $2w-k$. At $w=1$ a single inverse suffices. A monomial of total weight $W$ and $t\ge1$ positive-weight letters therefore costs at most $2W-t$ inverse powers of each gap. Powers of $A$ cost none. After Weyl averaging, multiplication by $\Delta^W$ clears these denominators. The symmetric polynomial coefficients are polynomials in the scalar traces. Subtract the resulting polynomial graviton from $\Delta^W X$. The difference is a classical cycle with identically zero Cartan restriction.

For this difference, filter the classical differential by the number of letters other than $A$. Its degree-preserving part pairs off-diagonal jet entries with their partners times $z_i-z_j$, with the nonzero constants of equations (101)–(103). Its degree-raising part replaces one positive-weight letter by two. The linear part vanishes on diagonal entries. On a monomial with off-diagonal entries, reverse the linear pairs by the graded derivation and divide by their total number of off-diagonal entries. The anticommutator of this inverse with the linear differential is the identity on that monomial. A degree-preserving inverse uses at most one inverse gap. At successive positive-letter counts $t=1,\ldots,W$, subtract the tree variation of the inverse applied to the lowest remaining piece. It is closed under the linear part. Its purely diagonal part vanishes because all earlier subtractions were tree boundaries and every tree boundary restricts to zero. At most $W$ inverses occur in any resulting coefficient.

Torus neutrality decomposes the colour indices of each monomial into directed cycles. Insert the spectral projector onto each index in the corresponding trace. A monomial with $t$ positive letters needs at most $t$ projectors, each with at most one additional pole at a specified gap. Pure eigenvalue factors remain coefficients. Thus at most $2W$ powers per gap suffice for this primitive. A further factor $\Delta^W$ clears them. Weyl averaging makes the remaining scalar coefficients symmetric, and density of regular semisimple $A$ gives the polynomial identity. All steps preserve the input weight, with the six first-flavour scalars from each discriminant factor and the one-letter shift prescribed by $Q_0$.

## Finite perturbative induction

In the source-notation order $(\theta_1,\theta_2,\theta_3;n_+,n_-)$, the undressed order-$m$ correction to R-007 has weight $(7-m,4-m,4-m;m,m)$ and length $10-m$. Multiplication by a discriminant at leading order adds six first-flavour scalars. The marked product at order $s$ removes $s$ of each flavour and adds $s$ to both dotted weights. Thus $W=8$ throughout. The project proves perturbative closure of the single gravitons and the Leibniz identity of the marked product. [Li–Liu–Chang, Theorem 9.1 and the quantum ring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24160)

Start with $\Delta^6F_0=Q_0 H_0$. Subtract $\mathbf QH_0$ from the fixed-bracketing marked product of six discriminants with $F$. The remaining closed cochain starts at order one. At an order $m\ge1$ take its leading coefficient $X$. It is $Q_0$-closed. The classical clearing bound supplies $\Delta^{16}X=G+Q_0H$. Multiply the entire remainder by the marked power $\Delta^{16}$ and subtract both the completed $G$ and $\mathbf QH$ at order $m$. The remainder now starts at order $m+1$. Products of earlier graviton terms with $\Delta^{16}$ are again in the graviton subalgebra. Homogeneous representatives and primitives may be chosen because each equation splits by weight and letter number. After $m=4$ a further term would have negative second and third flavour degrees. The induction stops with exponent $6+4\cdot16=70$.

## Test on a first instanton differential

Suppose the physical instanton filtration admits a matched product whose first differential on perturbative cohomology is a derivation, with homogeneous perturbative representatives of the discriminant and the single gravitons admitting closed physical completions. Equivalently for this test, the induction can use the perturbative limits of such closed generators with the same leading graviton terms and grading. This is the additional physical comparison. The formal quantum ring theorem supplies its perturbative part.

Under these hypotheses, writing $d_{\rm inst}$ for that differential, the preceding identity gives

$$
[\Delta]^{70}\star d_{\rm inst}[F]
=d_{\rm inst}\bigl([\Delta]^{70}\star[F]\bigr)=0 .
$$

Dot's explicit outgoing candidate $T$ is a graviton expression. On the regular Cartan point with scalar eigenvalues $(1,2,-3)$ and the two indicated scalar jets $(1,0,-1),(0,1,-1)$ its value is $-14112/5$. The discriminant there is 400. Its dressed leading coefficient therefore evaluates to

$$
-\frac{14112}{5}\,400^{70}\ne0.
$$

It represents a nontorsion perturbative class. On the classical page the product is Cartan-visible. At every perturbative page all incoming boundaries have zero Cartan restriction by the discriminant and marked-product argument, and the product itself has a closed lift. This also excludes a boundary after inverting the perturbative parameter. Therefore the proposed image $d_{\rm inst}[F]=c[T]$ requires $c=0$ under the stated comparison. A general image must obey the torsion equation above. The calculation gives no nonzero instanton pairing.
