At the conformal origin, the separated product of the bare R-007 source, the supercurrent and the radial conjugate of Dot's outgoing candidate vanishes when all three insertions are evaluated on the classical one-instanton solution. This holds for both orientations. The physical projected matrix element still requires quantum fluctuations, operator matching and Ward contact terms. ## The separated classical-profile term

An SU(3) charge-one instanton has 24 independent fermionic collective coordinates. Sixteen are exact and eight enter the moduli action. At zero scalar vev a scalar profile begins in fermionic degree at least two. A Weyl fermion begins in degree at least one. A field strength has degree at least zero. These bounds follow from the bosonic solution with vanishing scalars and fermions and from Grassmann parity. Covariant spacetime differentiation preserves each lower bound. They apply to the conjugate fields and to an anti-instanton as well. [Instanton expansion and the 8N fermionic coordinates](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.06346)

All 6,139 source words have five scalars and five matter fermions. Their minimum degree is therefore 15. In each of the 5,080 target words, let the four multiplicities count letters with zero, one, two and three flavour indices. These letters are covariant jets of a gaugino, scalar, matter fermion and field strength respectively. The first gaugino letter uses one dotted Taylor index. In Dot's ordering the target length is nine, its total flavour degree is nine, and its total dotted degree is four. Hence

$$
n_0+n_1+n_2+n_3=9,\qquad n_1+2n_2+3n_3=9,\qquad n_0\le4,
$$

$$
n_0+2n_1+n_2=18-2n_0\ge10,\qquad 15+10=25>24 .
$$

Exactly one of the 5,080 trace words attains this lower bound. The product is zero in the exterior algebra before integration over the odd moduli, before the current is inserted, and before integration over positions, scale or gauge orientation. The moduli action has nonnegative Grassmann degree. The same inequality holds for any outgoing word with these three grades. ## A bosonic functional on the outgoing cohomology

Retain only the scalar $A=\phi^1$, the minus Taylor derivative $B$ of $\phi^2$, and the plus Taylor derivative $C$ of $\phi^3$, in the pinned divided-jet conventions. Exactly five trace words of the candidate survive. With the defining trace, their sum is

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\frac85\operatorname{Tr}(A^3)\operatorname{Tr}(A^2)
 [\operatorname{Tr}(B^2)\operatorname{Tr}(C^2)-(\operatorname{Tr}(BC))^2]\\
&+\frac{32}{15}\operatorname{Tr}(A^3)
 [(\operatorname{Tr}(AB))^2\operatorname{Tr}(C^2)
 -2\operatorname{Tr}(AB)\operatorname{Tr}(AC)\operatorname{Tr}(BC)
 +(\operatorname{Tr}(AC))^2\operatorname{Tr}(B^2)] .
\end{aligned}
$$

For diagonal traceless matrices the Gram determinant of $A,B,C$ for the trace form is zero, since the Cartan has dimension two. Substitution reduces the five words to

$$
\frac{56}{15}\operatorname{Tr}(A^3)\operatorname{Tr}(A^2)
 [\operatorname{Tr}(B^2)\operatorname{Tr}(C^2)-(\operatorname{Tr}(BC))^2].
$$

Choose the three diagonals $(1,1,-2)$, $(1,0,-1)$ and $(0,1,-1)$, in that order. This gives $(56/15)(-6)(6)(4-1)=-2016/5$. Equivalently this evaluates the coefficient with five $A$, two $B$ and two $C$ insertions. This is a linear functional on the polynomial at fixed grades. On the entire unrestricted candidate, setting all colour matrices proportional to any **one** fixed Cartan element gives zero. All 5,080 words have trace lengths $2,2,2,3$, and all 566 graded coefficients left after taking out the common trace factor cancel. The three diagonals are a polynomial evaluation of Taylor variables. The instanton profile calculation above uses zero scalar vev.

## Use in the physical Ward calculation

Cartan evaluation kills $Q_0$ boundaries and, on perturbative page cycles, all higher incoming boundaries. At fixed physical charges the chain spaces are finite. Over $\mathbb Q[[u]]$, with $u$ the perturbative parameter, consider the saturation of the image of the full perturbative differential. Its reductions at $u=0$ are generated by tree boundaries and the leading coefficients of power-divisible perturbative boundaries. The latter give incoming page boundaries modulo earlier ones. Our functional vanishes on all of them by the Cartan-image theorem. The quotient by the saturated image is finite free over the discrete valuation ring. Lifting the functional on a basis of this quotient gives a formal dual cocycle with the displayed leading term. [Cartan-image theorem](https://github.com/lbotao399-stack/quantum-lifts-results/blob/2e22109bc140b10e6a9bf70892dcf6b586510475/nonperturbative-lift/contributions/dot-cartan-image/PROOF.md)

After identifying the perturbative physical and formal complexes in the chosen renormalization scheme, one can test a leading instanton supercharge with this dual. If its first allowed coefficient is $K$ in that common frame, the leading pairing is the displayed functional applied to $K O_0$, with $O_0$ the specified R-007 source. A nonzero value would detect a cohomology image. Evaluation on Dot's candidate calibrates the functional. The required insertion on R-007 has yet to be calculated. For the three-scalar probe the two classical endpoints alone have minimum degree $15+18=33$.
