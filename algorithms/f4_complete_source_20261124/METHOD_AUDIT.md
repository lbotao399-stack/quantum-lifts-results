# Method audit under the no-projection instruction

The instruction is applied to the actual operations, not their names.

## Permitted fresh path

`albert_tensor_runner.cpp` acts with the complete native 26-dimensional
matrices on explicitly retained tensor slots. The two Albert corners are
defined from the archived integral cubic and the full inverse invariant form.
The exchange tensor is another fixed cubic contraction. Every external index
is contracted. The code performs finite alternating sums and exact scalar
contractions; it constructs no idempotent onto a colour or Taylor summand.

`verify_closed_product.py` constructs every coefficient equation and solves
their common rational kernel. `verify_closed_bilinears.py` retains the entire
60-column invariant bilinear complex, checks its full 20-row classical action,
and proves a quantum row identity. Solving a kernel and verifying its action
does not replace the domain with a selected ansatz or apply a projection.

The matched-product proof uses these actual separately closed factors. The
unique incoming Taylor type is a consequence of a full equivariant map's
highest-weight equations. No operator projecting an arbitrary product onto
that type is invoked. The supplementary rational eight-dimensional Ward
check is not needed by the new direct-factor argument.

The literal extension was reconstructed completely. Its first 3002 rows had
already completed through the direct Albert callback when the mixed replay
was stopped. Only those extension values were reused; the standalone
`rebuild_albert_extension.py` evaluated the remaining 15 rows and has no
baseline compiler or colour-selection operator in its call path. The saved
extension receipt makes this reuse explicit. Running that standalone command
without `--reuse-prefix` recomputes all 150850 entries from scratch.

## Stopped and excluded path

The inherited `BoundaryPlan.decorate` evaluated the old bank using adjoint
and Albert colour-subspace maps, together with complementary skew/symmetric
combinations. Expanding or renaming those idempotents would not remove the
methodological issue. That route was stopped. Its compiler, runners and
result receipts were moved outside this publication directory. Their hashes
are retained in `stopped_route_provenance.json`, solely to prevent confusion
with permitted calculations.

The historical outgoing graph rebuilder also calls
`exceptional_colour.projectors`. Its optional native-column replay is excluded.
The new outgoing command has no native-column option: it verifies only the
saved complete matrices, rational characters and literal Cartan Gram
determinants. No permitted fresh full order-3017 reconstruction is claimed.

The published full-rank theorem remains an attributed mathematical input with
its immutable pin and scope. Reading that theorem or using its rank bounds is
distinguished from newly applying its historical colour-subspace operations.
The new literal extension and exact closed-factor argument do not turn that
historical determinant into a freshly regenerated one.

The fixed archived invariant tensors above are used as vertices of complete
scalar contractions. They are not used to select a colour subspace of the
source or to replace a full map with its action on a selected summand. The
historical colour-selection decorators removed from this packet perform that
different operation explicitly.
