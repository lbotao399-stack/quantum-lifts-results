# Why the first symplectic relation dimension stays67

Notation is the native source notation: Sp(r)=USp(2r), and
`A=(0,0,(0,))` is the undifferentiated first scalar. The native weight is
`(3,3,2*r-1,0,0)`. The first relation degree is length `2*r+2`, one degree
before the fortuitous representatives. Pair paths and their Grassmann
signs are exactly those of `paired_relation_basis.py`.

## Statement

For every integer r>=4, the complete first trace-relation highest-weight
space in this degree has dimension67. Adding one `(A,A)` path induces an
isomorphism between this space at rank r and at rank r+1. It commutes
with the spin raising operator.

This is a statement about the first relation space. It does not establish
an all-rank chain map on the next two degrees, their observed188 and183
dimensions, the121 differential rank, or an all-rank fortuitous class.

## Why paired paths give the complete first relation space

The symplectic second fundamental theorem says that tensor relations are
generated by the Pfaffian relation on2r+2 fundamental slots, with arbitrary
contractions of the remaining slots. See Rubey-Westbury,
*Combinatorics of symplectic invariant tensors*, Section2, especially the
theorem stated on page2 and Theorem2.13:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.02586 . The corresponding categorical theorem
is also in Lehrer-Zhang, *The Brauer Category and Invariant Theory*,
Theorem4.8: https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.5889 .

Each adjoint letter is the symmetric fundamental tensor `J X` used by
the existing generator. Antisymmetrizing both slots of one such tensor
gives zero. Thus a nonzero Pfaffian relation needs at least2r+2 distinct
adjoint letters. At the first relation degree there are exactly2r+2
letters, so each has one marked slot. Matching the remaining slots pairs
the letters into r+1 paths of length two. This proves spanning after
projection to the graded letter-content space.

These paired recipes are also independent in the universal trace
covering. The marked matching which closes each pair separately produces
the product of its r+1 quadratic traces, with coefficient `(-1)^(r+1)`
before the existing primitive-row convention. Every other marked
matching joins at least two paths and has fewer trace factors. Distinct
nonzero graded pair monomials have distinct leading products of
quadratic traces. Ordering by trace number makes the coefficient map
triangular with nonzero diagonal. There are no further dependencies in
this first relation module.

No physical operator or identity generator is rescaled in this argument.
The sign only proves injectivity of the existing recipe map.

## Why multiplication by the AA path stabilizes at rank4

At length2r+2 and theta-one degree2r-1, there are exactly three gaugino
letters; all other letters are first-scalar letters, possibly with Taylor
derivatives. The total Taylor degree is3+3=6. Each gaugino has at least
one Taylor derivative. Therefore at most three scalar letters can carry
derivatives. There are at most

```
3 gauginos + 3 differentiated scalars = 6
```

letters different from A.

At rank s>=6 a pair monomial has s+1>=7 pairs. Without an AA pair, every
pair would require a non-A letter, contradicting the bound of six.

At rank s=5 there are six pairs. Avoiding AA would require exactly six
non-A letters, so every gaugino and every differentiated scalar must have
exactly one Taylor derivative. All three gauginos are then of the two
types `(1,0,())` and `(0,1,())`. Every pair contains one A and one non-A
letter, so at least two of the three odd A-gaugino pairs are identical.
Their graded product is zero. Thus every **nonzero** pair monomial at
rank5 contains AA as well.

Consequently adding AA is a bijection on the full pair-weight bases
between ranks r and r+1 for every r>=4. The same argument applies after
spin raising: total Taylor degree and gaugino count are unchanged. Since
the spin raising operator annihilates A, adding AA commutes with that
operator. It therefore gives a bijection of highest-weight kernels.

This is an all-rank counting argument, not an extrapolation of a finite
sequence of character values.

## Exact finite seed

At rank4 the native pair-weight space has318 basis monomials. Its spin
raising matrix has251 rows and318 columns. The supplied251-by-251
integer minor has determinant

```
-18704061680052940575554534075116849806169323060854784
```

and hence rank251. Therefore the highest-weight dimension is

```
318 - 251 = 67.
```

The program additionally checks the literal AA bijection and every
raising coefficient for ranks4->5,5->6,6->7. Rank3 is a genuine lower-rank
exception: its corresponding dimensions are309-244=65. No claim of67 is
made there.

## Reproduce

Install this directory as `extension/stability/` in the parent package:

```bash
python extension/stability/verify_stability.py
```

Alternatively pass `--base PATH` to the four-certificate package. The
verification uses the included pinned native modules and no original
discovery-workspace imports. It reconstructs the pair-weight bases,
the raising matrices, the exact seed determinant, and all displayed
transport checks. The completeness and all-rank argument are the proofs
above; the executable supplies their finite arithmetic certificate.
