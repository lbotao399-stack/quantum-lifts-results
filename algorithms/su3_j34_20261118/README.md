# The SU(3) level-34 source and its direct second-order action

The highest component of Kim, Lee, Lee and Song's $\mathcal O_{34}^{(3)}$ has BMN charges $(2J;q_1,q_2,q_3)=(6;8,5,4)$. Our archived expansion of their trace relations has 5,815 terms in the paper's fields. The component even under charge conjugation $C$ has 6,270. Subtracting that component from the original gives exactly one half of $Q_0$ on the archived 78-term primitive. All 910 terms of the difference cancel. Here $Q_0$ is the classical supercharge. [Published construction, §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20340v2#S4.SS1) · [Parity primitive](odd_parity_primitive.json.gz)

## Block restrictions over the rationals

Restrict every colour matrix to $SU(2)\times U(1)$. An abelian field has colour matrix $\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2)$ times its field coordinate. In the table, $\hat\phi^i$ and $\hat\psi_i$ are abelian scalar and fermion coordinates of flavour $i$. The extracted odd coordinate is placed on the left.

| Coefficient of the even source | Target $(2J;q_1,q_2,q_3)$ | Predecessor trace words | Boundary rank | Restricted trace terms |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $(\hat\phi^1)^3\hat\phi^2$ | $(6;5,4,4)$ | 99 | 28 | 0 |
| $(\hat\phi^1)^4$ | $(6;4,5,4)$ | 99 | 28 | 0 |
| $(\hat\phi^1)^3\hat\psi_3$ | $(5;4,4,4)$ | 197 | 47 | 368 |

Each traceless $2\times2$ letter is evaluated on the generic matrix with rows $(u,v)$ and $(w,-u)$ and independent coordinates for each species. Odd coordinates use exterior multiplication. The adjoint of $SL_2$ is the three-dimensional vector representation of $SO_3$. Its invariant tensors are generated by the metric and the alternating tensor. After polarization and specialization to the stated letter parities, products of traces of lengths two and three therefore span the predecessor space. This justifies testing all 197 listed predecessors in the mixed channel.

The mixed coefficient is closed. A 14-coordinate functional vanishes on every predecessor image and evaluates to $120{,}294{,}720$ on the mixed coefficient. It evaluates to $-20$ on the $SU(2)$ threshold operator $\mathcal O_{24}^{(2)}$ in the normalization of equation (3.6). Their quotient fixes the coefficient

$$
120{,}294{,}720/(-20)=-6{,}014{,}736.
$$

Adding $6{,}014{,}736\,\mathcal O_{24}^{(2)}$ to the mixed coefficient gives $Q_0$ of the supplied 30-term primitive in all 705 monomials of the difference. The paper's $-144{,}353{,}664$ in equation (4.4) refers to its full flavour-singlet contraction. Our coefficient selects the single component in the table. [Threshold and singlet contraction](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20340v2#S4.SS1) · [Boundary functional](certificate.json) · [Exact primitive](threshold_identity.json)

The separate Fortuity computation found a one-dimensional kernel of the two scalar tests on the two non-graviton classes at prime $67{,}108{,}859$. The 6,270-term representative lies in that kernel and its mixed pairing is $53{,}185{,}861$ modulo the same prime. It thus supplies a nonzero representative of that line, given the published column and parity counts. Its rational nonexactness already follows from the exact mixed pairing. [Public column and restriction calculation](https://github.com/davidsd/fortuity-ai/blob/4256f207986029540060fb44dd494746828f65ce/summary/fortuity-summary.tex#L418)

## Full-letter second-order action

Applying the existing $SU(3)$ operator $Q_2$ to the even representative in the parent colour group gives 140,526 formal trace terms with common denominator 60. Every term has length 10, even $C$, and doubled charges $(2J_1,2J_2,2q_1,2q_2,2q_3)=(5,5,16,10,8)$. The import intertwines the two classical rules on all seven BMN letters. Two complete single-word $Q_2$ images, with 87 and 372 terms, also agree with the Python trace rules. [Rational cochain](Q2.jsonl.gz) · [Calculation record](second_action.json)

On the Cartan, sorting the diagonal letters with their Grassmann signs and collecting trace products leaves 576 terms. Substitution of $\operatorname{diag}(u,v,-u-v)$ with independent even or odd coordinates for every species gives the zero polynomial. The next equation requires a first correction $\mathcal O_1$ satisfying $Q_0\mathcal O_1=-Q_1\mathcal O_0$, where $\mathcal O_0$ denotes the even source. The second obstruction is represented by $Q_2\mathcal O_0+Q_1\mathcal O_1$ on the second page. Both its first-correction freedom and its nonabelian target enter that test. [Cartan polynomial](Q2_Cartan.json)

## Replay

From the repository root, the first two commands use the Python standard library. The full-letter replay uses a C++17 compiler and the NumPy, SciPy and python-flint dependencies of the published SU(3) runtime. The compressed action stores one JSON array per line, with a rational coefficient followed by its ordered multitrace. The uncompressed SHA-256 is in the calculation record. [Runtime requirements](../su3_independent_prime_20261118/requirements.txt)

```sh
python3 algorithms/su3_j34_20261118/verify.py
python3 algorithms/su3_j34_20261118/match_threshold.py
python3 algorithms/su3_independent_prime_20261118/build_native.py
python3 algorithms/su3_j34_20261118/second_action.py --output /tmp/su3-j34-q2-replay
```
