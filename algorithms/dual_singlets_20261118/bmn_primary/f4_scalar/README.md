# An exceptional nine-scalar generator in $F_4$

At $j=2(E+J_L)=18$, the nine-letter BMN flavour singlet $(3,3,3)$ is one-dimensional over $\mathbb Q$. Its representative is a contraction of the invariant cubic of the 26-dimensional traceless Albert algebra. It is an indecomposable scalar class. Its outgoing and incoming perturbative supercharge ranks are zero at every positive order. The exact stress-tensor current products also exclude an incoming instanton pairing. ## Polynomial and rational non-exactness

Use the integral Jordan multiplication and trace metric of the archived Albert colour representation. On the traceless subspace the metric has diagonal entries $2,6,2,\ldots,2$ in its specified basis. The array `cubic` pairs the Jordan product of two vectors with a third by this metric. Form `lower` by contracting one slot of each of two cubics using the inverse metric. Interchange the second and third open slots, then raise all four slots with the inverse metric to obtain `upper`. The following expression is a rational polynomial in the three scalar matrices acting on the traceless representation. Parenthesized subscripts indicate which of the four tensor factors the scalar acts on. The sign is that of the indicated permutation of the three flavours.

$$
\left\langle\texttt{lower},\,
 \left(\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in S_3\\\text{flavour permutations}}}
  \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\,
  \phi^{\sigma(1)}_{(1)}\phi^{\sigma(2)}_{(2)}\phi^{\sigma(3)}_{(3)}
 \right)^3\texttt{upper}\right\rangle .
$$

The pairing evaluates the four covariant slots on the four contravariant slots. All colour contractions are invariant. Each alternating triple transforms by the flavour determinant. The polynomial therefore has flavour type $(3,3,3)$. Its classical variation is zero since $Q_0\phi^i=0$. [Integral colour representation](../../f4_colour.py)

A product of positive-degree scalar classes at degree nine must include an odd-degree factor. The Littlewood–Richardson rule requires each factor partition to fit inside $(3,3,3)$. Of the nine eligible odd partitions at degrees $1,3,5,7$, only $(1,1,1),(3,1,1),(3,3,1)$ have nonzero scalar cochain spaces. Their dimensions and their incoming ranks are respectively $1,2,4$. Thus all nine factor cohomologies vanish. Products or positive BMN raising operations involving nonscalar letters cannot contribute at scalar length equal to the total flavour degree. This proves indecomposability in the campaign's BMN algebra. [All nine factors at the first prime](channels_1000003.json) · [Second prime](channels_1000033.json)

The central Weyl element acts by simultaneous sign reversal, so the polynomial vanishes on the Cartan. Any three commuting complex elements lie in a Borel subalgebra, since their span is abelian. A regular dominant Cartan cocharacter contracts its nilradical to zero. An invariant polynomial on such a triple consequently takes its Cartan value. At maximal length the boundary ideal is generated by the scalar commutators $Q_0\psi_i$. Taking invariants is exact on the homogeneous finite-dimensional pieces. The Nullstellensatz therefore makes this nonzero class nilpotent in the invariant coordinate ring of the commuting **scheme**. The least vanishing power has not been determined. ## Full-letter quantum partners

In the campaign's doubled charge order $(2J_1,2J_2,2q_1,2q_2,2q_3)$ the scalar has charge $(0,0,6,6,6)$. Every Taylor letter contains at least one even or odd Taylor slot. For an order-$r$ outgoing map the target would have $9-r$ slots in $10-r$ letters. An incoming perturbative map at $r>0$ requires derivative degrees $(-r,-r)$. This proves survival to each finite perturbative page directly from the supercharge grading. [Supercharge and physical gradings](../../../../supplement/supplement.tex)

For a physical incoming singlet the charge is $(1,1,6,6,6)$. The derivative degrees must be equal. At common degree $t$, the three flavour degrees are $3-t$ and the length is $8-t$. The table exhausts the source, including derivatives and gauginos.

For the six-letter sector take the two products of exact physical stress-tensor currents in the order of the archived component calculation. Octonion derivations act on the imaginary octonions in each of the three off-diagonal Albert entries. On this embedded $G_2$ each nonempty Albert trace is three times its trace in the seven. Both products contain three bilinears. Evaluating the full Albert trace on the two commuting jets gives

Every classical boundary vanishes on those jets, so the two products attain the cohomology upper bound $30-17-11=2$. Under the supersymmetric primary-product theorem both have exactly closed completions. They exhaust the possible incoming sources, including any mixing on later pages. The outgoing physical charge would have $2J_1=2J_2=-1$, which is incompatible with the allowed Taylor letters. This rules out this scalar as a nonperturbative lift under the exact-current and primary-product input. The calculation uses no instanton integral. ## Application at level 24

At BMN weight $(4,4,4)$, two new classical images increase the length-eleven outgoing rank from 59 to 61. The first is the nine-scalar polynomial times the trilinear commutator trace. The second inserts the matched scalar and its matter-fermion variation into the first tensor factor of the same contraction. In each case replacing the commutator by the corresponding matter fermion supplies the preimage. The augmented minor has determinant 672682 modulo 1000003. The squared determinant of the scalar trace Gram matrix is nonzero on a commuting Cartan triple, so at least one of the 62 terminal cochains survives. The outgoing rank is exactly 61. At length ten, sixteen field-strength insertions reproduce rank 79. With two scalar–matter-fermion insertions the rank increases to 80. In one-based tensor-slot order the independent row uses the pairs $(2,2)$ and $(1,4)$ followed by two alternating scalar triples. Applying the graded Leibniz rule to the two odd pairs gives the difference of the two substituted brackets. The augmented minor has determinant 379098 modulo 1000003. Thus the eleven-letter source has rational cohomology dimension $141-80-61=0$. Under the filtered superconformal selection rule this excludes the incoming $d_2$ into the fifth gaugino power.
