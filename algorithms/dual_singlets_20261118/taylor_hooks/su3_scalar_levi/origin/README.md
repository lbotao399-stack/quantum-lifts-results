# The first equation near the zero scalar triple

Let $\mathfrak m$ be the ideal generated by the entries of the three undifferentiated scalar matrices. The classical supercharge $Q_0$ kills these entries. The coefficient of degree $k$ in $\mathfrak m$ contains $k$ undifferentiated scalars. Taylor derivatives of scalars are independent variables. The following equations hold on unrestricted three-colour matrices.

| $SU(3)$ BMN level | Lowest scalar degree of $Q_1\mathcal O_0$ | Original terms at this degree | Correction terms | Graded four-slot trace identities | Exact residual in the quotient |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 32 | 5 | 79453 | 93351 | 16 | 0 modulo $\mathfrak m^6$ |
| 34 | 4 | 87234 | 108565 | 150 | 0 modulo $\mathfrak m^5$ |

Both lowest components contain the two first spacetime Taylor coefficients of the gauge ghost. The classical split of a differentiated matter letter interchanges a ghost coefficient with its scalar or fermion, with the graded sign. Splitting the mixed second ghost coefficient interchanges the two ghosts. In a product with both derivatives already assigned to ghosts, every other term in these variations has higher scalar degree. Applying the original signed differential to each predecessor checks this property. Moving the two ghosts into a fixed cyclic order by these interchanges leaves 32 and 650 trace terms, respectively.

Antisymmetrizing four defining colour indices gives zero in three colours. Expand this antisymmetrizer on the two individual ghost matrices and two ordered matter words. The 24 permutations separate into trace cycles, with the additional sign of the permutation of the odd matrix coefficients. At level 32 a combination of 16 such identities and the graded interchanges removes all 32 remaining terms. At level 34 we also use 142 ordinary classical variations. All inputs and all correction coefficients are rational. [Level-32 relations](j32_relations.json) · [Level-34 relations](j34_relations.json)

The final replay starts from the full signed first image and computes $Q_0$ on every term of the proposed correction. Subtracting the specified finite-colour trace identities leaves 425208 terms of degree six and 104885 of degree seven at level 32. At level 34 the surviving counts are 664246 at degree five and 308273 at degree six. Both corrections are gauge-invariant trace polynomials. The higher scalar layers govern extension to a global first correction. [Level 32](j32_full_replay.json) · [Level 34](j34_full_replay.json)

From the repository root use the Taylor-letter runtime shipped with the level-30 calculation and Python 3.10 or later.

```sh
d=algorithms/dual_singlets_20261118
p="$d/taylor_hooks/su3_scalar_levi/origin"
for j in 32 34; do
  python3 "$d/su3_scalar_origin_identities.py" --level "$j" --data "$p" || exit 1
done
```

Adding `--generate` reconstructs the two primitives from the short relation files. Adding `--next-residual` with a filename saves every surviving trace coefficient.
