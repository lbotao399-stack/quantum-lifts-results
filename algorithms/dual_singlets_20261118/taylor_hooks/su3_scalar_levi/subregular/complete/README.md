# The full rank-one chart at SU(3) levels 32 and 34

For each published BMN source $\mathcal O_0$, the complete first image $Q_1\mathcal O_0$ is now expanded in the rank-one scalar chart. The positions $23$ and $31$ in its odd matrix are the fields $f$ and $g$. Write $(f,g)$ for the ideal generated by all their Taylor coefficients. The signed classical equations $Q_0f=3af$ and $Q_0g=-3ag$ preserve this ideal and its square. Here $a$ is the $11$ entry of the same matrix. [Chart, sign convention and original letters](../README.md)

| Level | Original trace words | Distinct longest traces | Full matrix monomials | Monomials modulo $(f,g)^2$ | Terms of the rational correction | Residual modulo $(f,g)^2$ |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 32 | 331201 | 104825 | 253788 | 52153 | 32343 | 0 |
| 34 | 517468 | 229290 | 880876 | 166368 | 94148 | 0 |

Each input word contains seven coefficients of the even matrix and four of the odd matrix. Their polynomial degrees on the chart are at most one and two. Hence $7+2\cdot4=15$ bounds the full expansion. The nonzero counts by polynomial degree are

Moving a longest trace to the front of a trace product gives its graded permutation sign. Grouping all words with the same extracted trace allows the other factors to be added before matrix multiplication. The native expansion uses checked signed integer additions and multiplications. The original through-degree-nine expansions agree coefficientwise at both levels. At level 34 the independent A8 expansion also agrees in every coefficient of degrees ten and eleven. [Level 32](j32_image.json.gz) · [Level 34](j34_image.json.gz) · [A8 coefficient digests](../normal/a8_expansion.json)

The odd field $d$ is the $21$ matrix entry. Its equation $Q_0d=fg$ makes each $d$ coefficient a closed spectator modulo $(f,g)^2$. For each monomial in these spectators we solved $Q_0\mathcal O_1=-Q_1\mathcal O_0$, retaining both the constant and linear terms in $f,g$ in the same system. The replay multiplies the assembled correction by the classical differential on the original chart coordinates and clears the common denominator, 3 at level 32 and 120 at level 34. This proves the first equation in the quotient. The global invariant first correction and the higher supercharge obstructions remain open. [Level-32 correction](j32_fg_linear_primitive.json.gz) · [Level-34 correction](j34_fg_linear_primitive.json.gz)

From the repository root the integer replay requires Python 3.10 or later.

```sh
d=algorithms/dual_singlets_20261118
p="$d/taylor_hooks/su3_scalar_levi/subregular/complete"
for j in 32 34; do
  python3 "$d/su3_subregular_chart_replay.py" --image "$p/j${j}_image.json.gz" --primitive "$p/j${j}_fg_linear_primitive.json.gz" --roots 1 --out "/tmp/chart-$j.json"
  cmp "/tmp/chart-$j.json" "$p/j${j}_fg_linear.json" || exit 1
done
```

To regenerate a complete image, run `su3_subregular_trace_expansion.py --level 34 --work /tmp/trace34 --workers 2 --out /tmp/full34.json.gz` from the algorithm directory. Adding `--roots 1` gives the image for `su3_subregular_root_coupled.py`. Run the latter with `--image`, `--work` and each of `--d-count 0` through `--d-count 3` to reconstruct the quotient correction.

## A common correction for the two filtrations

Count the Taylor coefficients of the odd field $d$ in each matrix monomial. Only $Q_0d=fg$ lowers this count. Each such term introduces two coefficients of $f,g$. All other variations preserve the number of $d$ coefficients and preserve or increase the total $f,g$ degree. In particular, modulo $(f,g)^2$ every monomial in the $d$ coefficients is a separate spectator.

First solve in descending $d$ count with the full $f,g$ dependence. Leave all coefficients of this correction intact. From the previously verified correction modulo $(f,g)^2$, add the components with $d$ count below the lowest count already solved. The added terms have no effect on the solved high counts. At $f,g$ degree zero and one, the higher and lower components solve independent equations. The resulting single rational correction has the following integer replay in the untruncated chart.

| Level | Lowest solved $d$ count | Terms in high-count correction | Terms in combined correction | Common multiplier | Support of the surviving residual | Terms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 32 | 1 | 87340 | 93362 | 120 | zero $d$ count, $f,g$ degree 2 through 4 | 153527 |
| 34 | 1 | 234227 | 252847 | 11520 | zero $d$ count, $f,g$ degree 2 through 4 | 499085 |

The three surviving counts are 40184, 67248 and 46095 at level 32, and 134238, 217809 and 147038 at level 34. These residuals belong to the particular corrections displayed here. A nonzero obstruction would require testing their images in the full quotient by correction freedom. [Level 32](j32_joined.json) · [Level 34](j34_joined.json) · [Exact combination](../../../../su3_subregular_join_corrections.py)

From the repository root, recreate each combined polynomial by passing `--primitive` with an output path. Add `--residual` with another output path to save its signed residual and common denominator.

```sh
d=algorithms/dual_singlets_20261118
p="$d/taylor_hooks/su3_scalar_levi/subregular/complete"
for j in 32 34; do
  python3 "$d/su3_subregular_join_corrections.py" --image "$p/j${j}_image.json.gz" --high "$p/j${j}_high_d_primitive.json.gz" --linear "$p/j${j}_fg_linear_primitive.json.gz" --through-d 1 --out "/tmp/joined-$j.json"
  cmp "/tmp/joined-$j.json" "$p/j${j}_joined.json" || exit 1
done
```
