# First supercharge obstructions on the repeated-eigenvalue scalar locus

In undeformed $SU(3)$ SYM, take the charge-conjugation-even BMN representatives $\mathcal O_0$ at physical levels 32 and 34. Write $Q_0$ for the classical supercharge and $Q_1$ for its first perturbative coefficient. For either source the equation $Q_0\mathcal O_1=-Q_1\mathcal O_0$ is soluble when the first scalar $S$ is conjugate to $x\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2)$, with arbitrary other three-colour matrices. The following explicit homogeneous trace polynomials solve it on the centralizer. A finite contraction below extends the solution to the other matrix entries on this scalar locus. The global first differential remains open for both sources. The scalar-wall construction below takes $x\ne 0$. ## Polynomial corrections on the centralizer

Write every other matrix on the centralizer as $A=\operatorname{diag}(B+a\mathbf1_2,-2a)$, with $\operatorname{tr}_2 B=0$. Its scalar coefficient $a$ has the parity of $A$. Every classical variation is a graded commutator, so the $a$ variables are closed. In the upper block the variation of $B$ is the same classical split applied to the traceless two-colour variables. Group the equations by their ordered monomial in the $a$ variables, keeping exterior coefficients to the left.

The adjoint of $SL_2$ is the three-dimensional orthogonal representation. Its multilinear invariants are generated by pair contractions and the alternating volume tensor. A pair of volume tensors reduces to a determinant of pair contractions. Thus products of pair traces with at most one triple trace span the parent invariants. Polarization and antisymmetrization give the same spanning rule for repeated even and odd variables. Every reconstructed rational solution is checked on all polynomial coefficients in the three entries of each traceless $B$.

At level 32 the nonzero right-hand sides occur at scalar powers 3 and 4. They require 230 and 179 terms in the returned block representatives. At level 34 the returned counts at powers 2, 3 and 4 are 435, 1212 and 66. All higher powers have zero right-hand side on the centralizer. [Block solver](../../su3_levi_equation.py) · [Level-32 block certificates](j32/) · [Level-34 block certificates](j34/)

For ordered matrices $A_i,A_j,A_k$, with $i,j,k$ labelling the letters and the bracket denoting the graded commutator, the two trace identities used to extend a block representative are

$$
\operatorname{tr}_2(B_iB_j)=\operatorname{tr}_3(A_iA_j)-6a_i a_j,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}_2(B_iB_jB_k)=\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}_3\!\big(A_i[A_j,A_k\}\big).
$$

If $W=A_1\cdots A_n$, its lower diagonal entry is $(-2)^n a_1\cdots a_n$. At each scalar power $m\ge3$ in these equations,

$$
x^m a_1\cdots a_n=
\frac{x^m\operatorname{tr}_3 W-x^{m-1}\operatorname{tr}_3(SW)}{3(-2)^n}.
$$

At $m=2$ the numerator is $\operatorname{tr}_3(S^2W)-x^2\operatorname{tr}_3 W$, with the same denominator. The remaining powers use $x^2=\operatorname{tr}_3(S^2)/6$ and $x^3=-\operatorname{tr}_3(S^3)/6$. Direct substitution of the resulting $SU(3)$ words into the original first variations gives 13,342 and 44,426 terms after collecting upper-block trace words across the respective contents. Their polynomials in the four independent entries of each upper matrix vanish. [Trace extension and independent entrywise replay](../../su3_levi_extension.py)

## Arbitrary matrices at the same scalar

Keep $x\ne0$ fixed. Split $Q_0$ into the part $\delta$ that splits off one $S$ and the remaining part $D$. There are 15 pairs of non-scalar letters. In each pair the sign $s=\pm1$ is fixed by the ordered classical splits, with $\delta M=s[S,U]$ and $\delta U=0$ for the paired letters $M,U$. In an off-block entry from diagonal eigenvalue $\lambda_i$ to $\lambda_j$, this is $\delta M^i{}_j=s(\lambda_i-\lambda_j)U^i{}_j$, where $\lambda_i-\lambda_j=\pm3x$. Here $i,j$ specify the output and input components in the displayed block basis, and $\lambda_i$ is the eigenvalue at position $i$.

The odd derivation sending $U^i{}_j$ to $M^i{}_j/[s(\lambda_i-\lambda_j)]$ and every other generator to zero has anticommutator with $\delta$ equal to the count of this pair's entries. Sum the four off-block entries over all 15 pairs. On monomials of positive off-block degree, divide the resulting derivation by the total number of off-block factors. The resulting operator $h$ obeys

$$
\delta h+h\delta=1.
$$

This calculation respects the exterior signs and the traceless matrix constraints. The replay checks $Q_0^2=0$ on all 31 letters and verifies all 60 inverse coefficients. [Level 32](j32/normal.json) · [Level 34](j34/normal.json) · [Ordered-split replay](../../su3_scalar_wall_homotopy.py)

Let $R=Q_1\mathcal O_0+Q_0\mathcal O_1$ for the explicit centralizer correction above. Classical closure of the source and $Q_0Q_1+Q_1Q_0=0$ give $Q_0R=0$. Its restriction to block-diagonal matrices vanishes, so every monomial in $R$ contains an off-block entry. This ideal is preserved by $D$, since products of block-diagonal matrices are block diagonal. If $R_k$ is its lowest component in the number $k$ of non-scalar letters, then $\delta R_k=0$. Subtract $Q_0hR_k$ from $R$. The degree-$k$ component vanishes, and the new term $-DhR_k$ has degree $k+1$. This gives the correcting primitive by finite iteration.

Each non-scalar letter consumes at least one of the two spacetime degrees or the two other flavour degrees. Thus $k\le1+1+3+3=8$ at level 32 and $k\le1+1+4+3=9$ at level 34. At degree $k$ the coefficient of $R_k$ carries $x^{11-k}$, so $hR_k$ carries $x^{10-k}$. The smallest possible powers are $2$ and $1$, respectively. The resulting primitive is polynomial in $x$ and the other matrix entries for this fixed block decomposition. Conjugation transports the identity to the whole semisimple orbit. Constructing a primitive at a general three-colour scalar remains open.

For a direct check of the need for the off-block correction, multiply all off-block entries by $t$. For the two rational trace polynomials in the table, the recorded residual coefficients are

## Restriction to the pure two-colour subalgebra

Put every letter in the traceless upper $2\times2$ subalgebra, including the first scalar. At the physical first target the two classical highest-weight cohomologies over the rationals have dimensions

$$
639-457-182=0,\qquad 2179-1381-798=0.
$$

The nonzero adjacent minors give lower bounds on the rational ranks. Their sum attains the exact cochain dimension. In the same frames the explicit $SU(3)$ first images have respectively 191 and 292 nonzero coordinates, and appending them to the incoming matrix leaves its rank at 457 and 1381. ## The full two-colour block and the nilpotent scalar locus

Allow every letter, including the first scalar, to vary freely in the complexified Lie algebra of $S(U(2)\times U(1))$. Write it as $\operatorname{diag}(B+a\mathbf1_2,-2a)$ with $\operatorname{tr}_2B=0$. The classical variation acts on $B$ by the two-colour commutators and kills each spectator $a$. Hence the classical complex is the tensor product of the two-colour complex and the free supercommutative algebra of spectator jets. Negative transposition is inner on the traceless two-colour matrices and sends every spectator to its negative. A coefficient of spectator length $n$ has colour parity $(-1)^n$.

For each active flavour and dotted-spin highest weight, multiply its Weyl numerator by the spectator character and extract the coefficient at the shifted total highest weight. This gives the number of copies at each active length. A separate calculation with the full two-colour endomorphism representation, including the identity matrix, gives the same total cochain dimensions. On every active factor compute the incoming classical rank and the rank formed by stacking the outgoing differential and Cartan evaluation. Subtracting both ranks from its cochain dimension bounds the dimension of the rational cohomology kernel of Cartan restriction. Every nonzero minor modulo the indicated prime supplies a rational lower rank bound. ### The level-34 first image

The two possible first-target directions both have seven active letters. In flavour and dotted-spin degree order their highest weights are $(4,4,2;1,1)$ and $(4,4,3;1,0)$. The corresponding rational upper bounds per copy are $346-149-196=1$ and $126-62-63=1$. Each occurs once.

The first flavour representation is the fourth determinant power times the dual of the symmetric square of the fundamental. Its weights subtract two units from $(4,4,4)$. At total flavour weight $(7,4,3)$ the four spectator scalars can therefore have multiplicities only $(4,0,0)$, $(3,1,0)$ or $(3,0,1)$. The second flavour representation is the fourth determinant power times the dual fundamental. Its four spectators comprise three first-flavour scalars and either component of the first gauge ghost derivative. These five coefficients exhaust the two possible representations at the total highest weight.

Expand all 517468 terms of the archived first image, with the odd spectator on the left. The three purely scalar coefficients vanish as trace polynomials. The other two each contain 36 cyclic trace terms and 654 nonzero two-colour matrix monomials. Every classical parent is spanned by products of three pair traces: a pair of three-colour-slot alternating tensors reduces to a determinant of pairings in the three-dimensional adjoint of $SU(2)$. At each spin component the ranks of the 487 parents and of the augmented matrix on 1001 independent cochain coordinates are both 334 modulo $1000003$. Each rational correction has 16 terms. Multiplying each by the classical differential cancels all 654 monomials exactly. Thus the level-34 first image also vanishes in cohomology on the full Levi. The earlier negative-image calculation reproduced 18 terms after imposing the two-colour trace reversal on the minus component. ### Arbitrary matrices above a scalar with a simple eigenvalue

Let $S$ be a traceless three-colour scalar with a simple eigenvalue $s$. In its invariant splitting write $S=\operatorname{diag}(B,s)$, where $B-s\mathbf1_2$ is invertible. This includes a size-two Jordan block. Extend a polynomial Levi primitive to the other letters by applying the block-diagonal projection to each letter. The remaining closed residual vanishes when the off-block entries are set to zero.

In the part $\delta$ of $Q_0$ that splits off one $S$, the off-block maps are left multiplication by $B-s\mathbf1_2$ and right multiplication by $s\mathbf1_2-B$, with the signs of the 15 classical pairs above. Their inverses define the contracting derivation. Its anticommutator with the on-block part of $\delta$ vanishes, and its anticommutator with the off-block part counts off-block entries. Division by that positive integer contracts the ideal. The rest of $Q_0$ preserves the ideal and raises the number of non-$S$ letters. Subtraction in successive degrees terminates at fixed Taylor weight. The inverse matrices and the two spectral projectors are regular on the chart with this simple eigenvalue, so the constructed primitive transforms by conjugation on that chart.

A traceless $3\times3$ matrix with no simple eigenvalue is nilpotent. Flavour changes commute with the colour restriction, and the vanishing on the full Levi extends to each flavour multiplet of the specified images. Consequently a remaining global first obstruction at either level can have support only where every linear combination of the three scalars is nilpotent. The same support bound holds for the level-34 second residual if its global first equation is soluble. A global polynomial primitive or a nonzero global obstruction still requires the calculation on this locus.
