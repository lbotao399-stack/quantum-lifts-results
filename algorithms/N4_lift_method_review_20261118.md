## What to keep and what to change

The manuscript supplies the operators $Q_n$, the order-$n$ terms of the perturbative supercharge, on letters with arbitrary holomorphic derivatives. The supplement already gives the coupled rank formula on successive pages $E_r$, where $E_1=H(Q_0)$ is classical cohomology and $d_r$ is the induced order-$r$ obstruction. Keep this formula and its normalization. The changes concern how to find representatives, choose target sectors and certify the action. Interpreting the quantized twist as the physical perturbative local-operator complex uses the comparison stated in Appendix B. ## The coupled calculation at $SU(3)$ level 30

For the published charge-conjugation-odd $SU(3)$ source at $j=30$, write $\mathcal O_0$ for its representative and $\mathcal O_1,\mathcal O_2$ for the first two corrections. The two equations are

$$
Q_0\mathcal O_1=-Q_1\mathcal O_0,\qquad
Q_0\mathcal O_2=-Q_2\mathcal O_0-Q_1\mathcal O_1 .
$$

| A8 solve modulo $1{,}000{,}033$ | Unknowns | Rank | Free coordinates | Nonzero production residuals |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| First equation | 28,311 | 18,003 | 10,308 | 0 of 34,901 |
| Both equations together | 77,639 | 50,147 | 27,492 | 0 of 100,307 |

The two returned solutions differ on **10,015 of the 10,308** free first-correction coordinates and on **28,018** first-correction coordinates overall. This comparison measures which coordinates the solver chose. Whether the original particular solution also admits some extension requires a separate fixed-solution rank test. In the $Sp(2)$ $j=30$ control, two directions detected by the direct $Q_3$ term disappear after adding both lower-correction terms. The three modular ranks are $2638,2638,2638$. Test the complete descent at both orders. [SU(3) solution arrays and ordered pivot check][receipt] · [Numerical record][numbers]

At order three the simultaneous equations for the corrections are

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
Q_0&0&0\\
Q_1&Q_0&0\\
Q_2&Q_1&Q_0
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}\mathcal O_1\\\mathcal O_2\\\mathcal O_3\end{pmatrix}
=-
\begin{pmatrix}Q_1\mathcal O_0\\Q_2\mathcal O_0\\Q_3\mathcal O_0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Here $\mathcal O_3$ is the third correction. A certificate for a $d_3$ lift must give a nonzero class on $E_3$, a solution of the first two rows, and a functional that kills the full three-row correction matrix and pairs nontrivially with the right-hand side. The corresponding $d_2$ certificate uses its first two rows and verifies the source on $E_2$. If the source space has several classes, quotient the incoming differentials and retain all source combinations when computing the rank.

### 4. Decide whether the $G_2$ instanton calculation is warranted

In the doubled physical-charge order $(2J_1,2J_2,2q_1,2q_2,2q_3)$, the incoming source has charges $(1,1,4,4,4)$, the target $(0,0,4,4,4)$. At lengths $3,4,5$ the source cohomology dimensions are $(0,1,0)$. At lengths $4,5,6$ the target dimensions are $(1,0,1)$. The middle source dimension is $5-1-3=1$. Its 18-term representative has a perturbative completion. The possible image has two coordinates, along the square of the $G_2$ gaugino bilinear $S$ and the scalar Gram determinant. Both coordinates are uncomputed. [Exact ranks, correction and six-product identity][g2-check] · [Charge record][numbers]

The two classical target projectors now have explicit rational coefficients. Each is normalized to one on the corresponding archived representative and vanishes on every archived boundary, independently on both evaluation frames. In the specified seven-dimensional trace convention, the Cartan exterior coefficient of $[\operatorname{tr}_7(\lambda_+\lambda_-)]^2$ is $-96$, while the scalar Gram determinant restricts to zero. The target Cartan restriction has rank one and kernel of dimension one; the pair of projectors still requires a physical instanton insertion.
