# Scope of the 100-point programme

Version 1.0, fixed on 2026-11-24. The machine-readable companion is [scope.json](scope.json).

## What 100 points means

One hundred points means that all sixty declared deliverables have passed their acceptance tests. The programme combines a large finite census, explicit unbounded-family theorems, physical operator/current matching, and a controlled chaos investigation. It does **not** mean classification at every gauge rank, every charge, every perturbative order and every instanton number. No inspected result gives a finite cutoff for that entire infinite problem.

“Almost all” has an exact denominator only after a search universe is frozen. Below, it means at least **99% by nonzero charge blocks and 99% by classical cohomology dimension**, with every omitted block listed. The uniform-family gates separately address infinitely many ranks or charges. There is no probability attached to these thresholds.

The coverage definition is frozen now. The explicit expanded block list and its hash are still missing and belong to A07. Until exact dimensions have been computed for every admitted block, no numerical percentage of discovery or lift coverage may be claimed.

## Theory and operator space

The target theory is **undeformed four-dimensional N=4 super Yang–Mills theory**, in the repository's one-sixteenth-BPS supercharge convention. Algebraic stages use the normalized relative holomorphic Taylor-jet complex specified by the pinned N4 input/manuscript. Keep every allowed letter, all holomorphic derivatives permitted by the charge bound, and every admissible correction cochain.

The repository's physical level is **twice the sum of energy and left spin**. Preserve each package's actual coordinate ordering; the Sp3 relation package and the usual three-flavour/two-jet tuple do not place all five entries in the same order. The common exchange format must translate them explicitly.

Targets are enumerated at every allowed set of native conserved charges and every cochain length, with full symmetry multiplicities recorded. A highest-weight calculation must include a completeness proof for the represented multiplet. Descendants remain present in the operator space; identifying independent primaries is a separate, certified classification step. A zero signed index does not remove a block.

No projection construction, selected subcomplex, omitted homogeneous correction, or unproved quotient by translations is an allowed substitute for the complete problem. A proof that a block is absent is allowed and must be retained as evidence. Physical gates additionally require the actual renormalized operators, positive inner product and radial-adjoint conditions; formal cohomology alone does not supply them.

## Core full-jet census

For each group below, include **every target block through physical level 36, inclusive**, and all adjacent/correction spaces needed to decide it, even if a source's displayed grading lies outside the target cutoff.

The symplectic label is the rank; the defining representation has twice that dimension. SO(5) is not counted separately from Sp(2). No new SU(2), Sp(1) or SO(3) scans are included.

This is an ambitious future frontier, not a statement that the current engines can already finish it. It includes full derivatives. The smaller SU3 level-30/SU4 level-28 certification milestone and the Sp3/G2 derivative-free level-30 milestone supply intermediate completion tests.

### Coverage arithmetic

First compute exact classical cohomology dimensions for **100%** of admitted blocks. Then publish four integers:

1. Number of nonzero classical blocks with the required complete decision.
2. Total number of nonzero classical blocks.
3. Sum of classical cohomology dimensions in those fully decided blocks.
4. Total classical cohomology dimension in all admitted blocks.

For B10, a decision includes both fortuity and the complete proper-product quotient. For D10, it includes the final perturbative fate of **every** class in that block: its first nonzero incoming/outgoing pairing or complete survival. A partially resolved block contributes zero to the resolved numerator, even if one famous class is understood. Both ratios must meet the 99% threshold. Zero blocks are reported separately and never inflate a nonzero denominator. An empty denominator is recorded as empty, not as a successful percentage.

Use exact integer comparisons: one hundred times each resolved count must be at least ninety-nine times its total. Resolve every named frontier target below regardless of how small its weight is in the aggregate census.

## Physical nonperturbative coverage

The first complete matrix target is **instanton charge one** for all full-jet SU3 target blocks through level 36 and SU4 target blocks through level 28, plus the named G2 third-power and F4 fifth-power channels. Include every symmetry-compatible incoming/outgoing physical partner even when it lies outside the target cutoff. Use only fully specified perturbative survivors, with physical operator mixing and normalization fixed.

Resolve a coefficient only after including the complete microscopic current insertion, measure/action terms, contact terms, operator mixing and relevant integration boundaries. A long-multiplet anomalous dimension, a selected zero-mode saturation, a localised topology, an effective-superpotential derivative or a formal polynomial primitive does not determine this physical supercharge matrix.

E08 goes beyond this finite charge-one window: it requires charges two and three **and an all-instanton theorem** for one explicitly fixed nontrivial nonscalar family. Start with the R-007 family if its physical channel is nontrivial; otherwise choose the first eligible family from the frozen physical partner census and record that choice before evaluating higher charges. Finite low-instanton zeros alone do not pass.

A complete physical no-lift theorem is a valid scientific resolution of its declared scope. It does not count as discovery of a nonzero nonperturbative lift. The global existence question remains separate whenever the theorem covers only a proper sector.

## BPS-chaos contract

The target is a physical diagnostic within **complete charge and symmetry blocks that contain fortuitous survivors**, not the size or spectrum of an arbitrary cochain matrix. The BPS Hamiltonian is degenerate at fixed charges; a different, physically defined observable is required. [Chen–Lin–Shenker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.19387v4) provides motivation and protected-sector comparisons, not an already completed calculation in this repository's fortuitous sectors.

G01 must establish a permitted direct-correlator diagnostic with explicitly constructed physical external states, or an intrinsic cohomology endomorphism with a justified connection to the diagnostic. These are separate possible constructions; their equivalence is not assumed. No projection construction is part of this plan.

Use two independently defined simple physical probes. Resolve continuous, discrete and antiunitary symmetries. Do not discard matrix elements between monotone and fortuitous classes merely because their rank classifications differ: that classification has not been proved to be a probe-preserved symmetry.

Before inspecting target spectra, freeze the hypothesis, spectral normalization, windows, controls, numerical error requirements and multiple-testing procedure. The version-1 calibration target is a false-positive probability at most **1/100** and power at least **9/10** against the **specified competing finite models**. These are design requirements, not already established statistical facts. Determine necessary block sizes by this calibration; do not count formal cochains as physical states.

The scaling gate requires at least three distinct non-Abelian ranks and three growing-charge blocks per rank, with both probes. These are minimum coverage requirements, not an asymptotic proof. A strong-chaos conclusion requires justified scaling information about long-range correlations and observable normalization. Bound omitted perturbative and nonperturbative contributions at the measured spectral scales, or restrict the conclusion to a fixed-rank weak-coupling limit. Numerical eigenvalue precision does not control missing physical corrections; a simultaneous large-rank, finite-coupling conclusion requires uniform physical error control. A positive spacing plot, a finite-rank fortuity certificate, and a large cochain matrix cannot establish that conclusion.

An exact or controlled negative conclusion can resolve the declared hypothesis. An inconclusive finite experiment may close the reproducible-test gate G04, but not the scaling or physical-mechanism gates G05/G06. The fortuity/black-hole correspondence remains a physical claim to test, as distinguished in [Chang–Lin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10129v3).

## Revisions

Changing a group list, level bound, instanton window, statistical criterion, weight or completion test creates a new rubric version. Keep the old manifest and old score; publish the new score on the same evidence and explain the difference. Extend the scientific question when new evidence warrants it. Never retroactively change a denominator or delete a failed sector to inflate progress.
