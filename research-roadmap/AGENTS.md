# Research roadmap workflow

The user's latest instructions take precedence. This roadmap is an editable research programme, not a restriction against a better scientific method. Existing nested experiment instructions continue to apply.

## At the start of a research task

1. Read `README.md`, `SCORECARD.md`, `CURRENT_STAGE.md` and the selected gate in `MILESTONES.md`.
2. Check the actual current GitHub head and any new evidence. The baseline score is a dated snapshot, not a live claim about uninspected later commits.
3. Select one principal milestone. State the exact next calculation and what result would pass its acceptance test. Dependencies block final acceptance where specified, not exploratory work.
4. Preserve complete native charge spaces, all derivatives allowed by the task, and all homogeneous earlier corrections. Do not introduce projections or silently truncate the complex. Preserve spinor indices in mathematical presentation when applicable.
5. Schedule no new SU2/Sp1/SO3 research scans under this programme. Historical controls can be cited with their scope intact.

## Evidence and scoring

- Separate an exact proof, a numerical certificate, a conditional theorem, a formal existence result, an explicit representative and a physical matrix element.
- Keep target counts, map ranks, multiplet counts, independent generators and product consequences separate.
- Complete a gate only after every acceptance condition holds. Attach immutable input/code/artifact pins, the exact command, a receipt, mathematical completeness evidence and an independent decisive check where required.
- Full-source zeros must include all admissible sources and correction freedom. Nonzero target coefficients must be nonzero in the required complete cohomology.
- After a result: update `evidence.json`, then the selected record in `milestones.json`; regenerate with `python3 research-roadmap/score.py --write`; validate with `python3 research-roadmap/score.py`; update the roadmap `README.md`, `CURRENT_STAGE.md`, `HISTORY.md` and dated root navigation consistently.
- Do not award points merely for proposing a framework, writing a document, adding a benchmark, or rerunning a known control. Record useful intermediate progress as `partial`, with zero points until the gate passes.
- Do not infer physical instanton lifting from formal perturbative survival, or chaos from a large algebraic matrix.

## Speculation and sharing

The user requires immature highly innovative work to remain isolated until a reliable pattern and independent checks exist. Do not read, delegate, quote, summarize or publish quarantined/HIGHLY_INNOVATIVE material as routine roadmap evidence. This public roadmap contains goals and established public evidence; it is not a release of private hypotheses. Publication authorization is determined by the user's instructions, not by this file.

## Revising the programme

If evidence changes the question, propose a concrete revised gate or scope and explain the scientific reason. Version changes to weights, scope or tests; preserve the old ledger and calculate old/new scores on the same evidence. Never silently relax a test, shrink a denominator, hide a failed sector, or rewrite a withdrawal to increase the score.

A valid negative result can close a decision gate. It cannot become a positive-lift or positive-chaos claim. If a theorem proves the originally sought phenomenon impossible in its complete domain, revise the question explicitly rather than continuing an impossible search.
