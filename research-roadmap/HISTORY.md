## Change protocol

An evidence-only update under unchanged tests keeps the rubric version and records the assessment date, input commit, newly met or reopened gate, exact evidence and score change.

A change to a weight, completion test, coverage universe or scientific question increments the rubric version. Preserve the previous JSON documents under `history/<version>/`, explain the reason, and show both old and new scoring on the same evidence. A withdrawn certificate reopens its gate unless another complete independent certificate still passes it. Do not edit the historical assessment to imply that a later correction was known earlier.
