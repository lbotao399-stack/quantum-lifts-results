# Nonperturbative Lifting and Quantum Operator Algebras

Supersymmetric local operators, exact products, and effects beyond perturbation theory

<a id="np-abstract"></a>

### Abstract

We study polynomial local operators, products of fields and their derivatives at one spacetime point, preserved by a chosen supersymmetry transformation relating bosonic and fermionic variables.

Two preserved operators represent the same class when their difference is a supersymmetry variation; we calculate exact products of these classes, including a nonzero class whose square survives and whose cube vanishes.

An identity expressing repeated multiplication as a supersymmetry variation gives a conditional mechanism for removing classes through effects beyond perturbation theory, the power-series expansion in the coupling.

The affected classes are built from explicit operators whose supersymmetry variations equal products already vanishing in cohomology, the space of preserved operators modulo such variations.

We evaluate how the gauge field inside differentiated operators changes under the supersymmetry transformation.

An instanton is a finite-action solution of the field equations in Euclidean spacetime, obtained by continuing time to imaginary values.

The remaining local equation compares the instanton-induced variation of an operator after its coincident-point singularities have been subtracted with the proposed operator on the other side of the pairing.

Known exact descriptions of theories containing matter determine polynomial constraints, several possible vacuum expectation values, interactions containing extra fermion factors, and removal of operators that create magnetic flux.

We track nonzero classes annihilated by a parameter, and specify where low-energy interactions containing inverse fields or fractional powers are defined.

In magnetic supersymmetric QCD we determine the entire scalar ideal removed by the determinant interaction. A second presentation of the same confined theory has fifteen quadratic generators for its removed ideal. We prove that sixteen microscopic current components survive every power of the loop parameter. The incoming current exhibits a failure of cohomology to commute with setting both mass and loop parameter to zero. Its proposed massive instanton matching requires a specific mass pole. In pure gauge theory with eight supercharges, a holomorphic translation identity protects two infinite families of local operators under the stated bulk deformation.

In the theory with sixteen independent supersymmetry transformations, operators unchanged by spacetime rotations and detected by exact zero-energy vacuum expectation values survive; a specific unprotected operator acquires a correction to the power governing its short-distance correlation.
