### $SP(N_c)$: Pfaffian, quantum constraint, and mass descent

The compact group $SP(N_c)$ preserves a nondegenerate antisymmetric color form.

Its mesons are antisymmetric in flavor.

For an antisymmetric matrix, the Pfaffian is a polynomial whose square equals the determinant, with sign fixed by the ordered flavor basis.

In the four-by-four convention already used for $V$,

$$
\operatorname{Pf}V=V^{12}V^{34}-V^{13}V^{24}+V^{14}V^{23}.
$$

In Intriligator–Pouliot notation, $SP(N_c)$ has fundamental dimension $2N_c$, with $2N_f$ chiral fundamentals.

Here $J^{ab}$ is the source symplectic color form, and $i,j$ are flavor component labels:

$$
M_{ij}=Q_{ia}Q_{jb}J^{ab}=-M_{ji},
\qquad
W_{\mathrm{mag}}=\frac1{4\mu}M_{ij}q_a^iq_b^jJ^{ab}.
$$

The magnetic group is $SP(N_f-N_c-2)$, with scale matching

$$
\Lambda_{N_c,N_f}^{3(N_c+1)-N_f}
\widetilde\Lambda_{N_f-N_c-2,N_f}^{3(N_f-N_c-1)-N_f}
=16(-1)^{N_f-N_c-1}\mu^{N_f}.
$$

The source mass and Higgs thresholds are, respectively,

$$
\Lambda_{N_c,N_f-1}^{3(N_c+1)-(N_f-1)}
=m\Lambda_{N_c,N_f}^{3(N_c+1)-N_f},
$$

$$
\Lambda_{N_c-1,N_f-1}^{3N_c-(N_f-1)}
=\frac2{M_{2N_f-1,2N_f}}
\Lambda_{N_c,N_f}^{3(N_c+1)-N_f}.
$$

In these normalizations, $\omega_{N_c+1-N_f}$ denotes the corresponding root of unity:

$$
W=(N_c+1-N_f)\omega_{N_c+1-N_f}
\left[
\frac{2^{N_c-1}\Lambda_{N_c,N_f}^{3(N_c+1)-N_f}}
{\operatorname{Pf}M}
\right]^{1/(N_c+1-N_f)}
\qquad (N_f\le N_c),
$$

$$
\operatorname{Pf}M
=2^{N_c-1}\Lambda_{N_c,N_c+1}^{2N_c+2}
\qquad(N_f=N_c+1),
$$

$$
W=-\frac{\operatorname{Pf}M}
{2^{N_c-1}\Lambda_{N_c,N_c+2}^{2N_c+1}}
\qquad(N_f=N_c+2).
$$

> **Proof: instanton in broken magnetic $SP(1)$.**
>
> Starting from electric $N_f=N_c+3$, add a mass for the last pair:
>
> $$
> q_a^{2N_f-1}q_b^{2N_f}J^{ab}=-2\mu m.
> $$
>
> The light magnetic mass matrix is $\widehat M/(2\mu)$, giving the source instanton term
>
> $$
> W_{\mathrm{inst}}
> =-\frac{\widetilde\Lambda_{1,N_c+3}^{3-N_c}\operatorname{Pf}\widehat M}
> {(2\mu)^{N_c+3}m}.
> $$
>
> Using $\Lambda_{N_c,N_c+3}^{2N_c}\widetilde\Lambda_{1,N_c+3}^{3-N_c}=16\mu^{N_c+3}$,
>
> $$
> W_{\mathrm{inst}}
> =-\frac{16}{2^{N_c+3}}
> \frac{\operatorname{Pf}\widehat M}{m\Lambda_{N_c,N_c+3}^{2N_c}}
> =-\frac{\operatorname{Pf}\widehat M}{2^{N_c-1}\Lambda_{N_c,N_c+2}^{2N_c+1}}.
> $$
>
> The next mass flow uses $\operatorname{Pf}M=M_{2N_f-1,2N_f}\operatorname{Pf}\widehat M$; the heavy equation gives the quantum constraint.
>
> Eliminating the heavy entry after the following mass flow gives the ADS pole.
>
> $\square$

For example, the three adjacent $SP(2)$ results are

$$
W_{N_f=4}=-\frac{\operatorname{Pf}M}{2\Lambda_{2,4}^5},
\qquad
\operatorname{Pf}M\big|_{N_f=3}=2\Lambda_{2,3}^6,
\qquad
W_{N_f=2}=\frac{2\Lambda_{2,2}^7}{\operatorname{Pf}M}.
$$

Magnetic rank-$2r$ patches yield an incompatible quantum constraint at $r=N_c+1$ and an ADS runaway at $r\ge N_c+2$, restoring $\operatorname{rank}\langle M\rangle\le2N_c$.

[Intriligator–Pouliot](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9505006)

### The same confined theory with two different zero-weight limits

**Proposition 18.1.** In the completely Higgsed $SP(1)$ magnetic description above, hold the entries of the antisymmetric light meson fixed. The entire scalar ideal killed by a power of the magnetic instanton weight consists of the polynomial combinations of the Pfaffians obtained by deleting two rows and the same two columns. The first power kills this ideal. In the $N_c=1$ symplectic theory with three remaining flavor pairs there are fifteen independent quadratic generators. The $SU(2)$ magnetic presentation of the same low-energy theory, with its light magnetic quarks held fixed, has the single cubic determinant generator of Proposition 16.2.

> **Proof.** The two heavy symplectic quarks span the two magnetic colors. Varying the mixed heavy–light singlets sets both components of each remaining quark to zero. The light fields are therefore the entries of $\widehat M$. For $i<j$ in the ordered flavor basis the derivative of the Pfaffian is $(-1)^{i+j+1}\operatorname{Pf}\widehat M_{\widehat i\widehat j}$, where the subscript deletes the indicated indices and indices start at one. Let $I$ be the ideal generated by these signed minors. Up to the fixed unit $-1/((2\mu)^{N_c+3}m)$, the F-term ideal over the polynomial parameter ring is $\widetilde\Lambda_{1,N_c+3}^{3-N_c} I$. Multiplication by the weight is injective on the quotient by $I$, since $I$ has parameter-independent generators. Its torsion in the original scalar quotient is precisely
>
> $$
> I\big/\bigl(\widetilde\Lambda_{1,N_c+3}^{3-N_c} I\bigr).
> $$
>
> This calculation also holds after parameter-adic completion. At six flavor coordinates the fifteen four-index Pfaffians are independent quadratics since each monomial identifies its four-index subset.
>
> At $SP(1)=SU(2)$ and three remaining flavor pairs, order the flavor slots as $(1,\tilde1,2,\tilde2,3,\tilde3)$. Use the source electric baryons $B_i,\widetilde B^{\tilde\jmath}$ and $V^{i\tilde\jmath}=\widehat M^i{}_{\tilde\jmath}$, with the cyclic ordered pairs $23,31,12$ carrying the three baryons in each flavor space. The Pfaffian with this ordering gives
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Pf}V=\det\widehat M-
> B_i\widehat M^i{}_{\tilde\jmath}\widetilde B^{\tilde\jmath}.
> $$
>
> On a fixed nonzero-weight fiber substitute $B_i=\sqrt{\Lambda_L^3/\mu}\,\widehat q_i$ and the same factor for $\widetilde B^{\tilde\jmath}$. Then
>
> $$
> -\frac{\operatorname{Pf}V}{\Lambda_L^3}
> =\frac{1}{\mu}\widehat q_i\widehat M^i{}_{\tilde\jmath}
> \widehat{\widetilde q}^{\tilde\jmath}
> -\frac{\widetilde\Lambda^2}{\mu^4m}\det\widehat M,\qquad
> \Lambda_L^3=\frac{\mu^4m}{\widetilde\Lambda^2}.
> $$
>
> The baryon coordinate factor uses an inverse square root of the magnetic weight. The zero-weight symplectic fiber is affine space of dimension $15$. In magnetic quark coordinates the zero-weight equation $\widehat q_i\widehat{\widetilde q}^{\tilde\jmath}=0$ makes one of the two vectors vanish at each point. Each one-sided incidence locus of Proposition 16.2 has dimension $9$. These special fibers therefore differ even in dimension. Their common nonzero-weight superpotential fixes neither special fiber without a choice of regular coordinates. $\square$

The exact check expands all fifteen signed Pfaffian derivatives and the scale substitution in this flavor order. [Calculation](../checks/scalar_saturation.py)

**Proposition 18.2.** Keep the ungauged holomorphic effective model of the light mesons with the displayed instanton Pfaffian superpotential. In its full polynomial jet complex every parameter-torsion class is a first-instanton image. At six flavor coordinates, among targets with exactly two undifferentiated scalar letters and $p-1$ undifferentiated odd partners there are exactly $\binom{15}{p}$ independent such images, for $1\le p\le15$. In particular, the first fermionic sector contains $105$ independent lifted operators.

> **Proof.** Use the antichiral convention $Q\beta=-\partial\bar W/\partial\gamma$, where $\gamma$ are the scalar coordinates. The only interaction vertices of this holomorphic meson model contain scalar letters. A positive-loop graph for the differential requires an edge between two interaction vertices, and every edge needs an odd terminal. Hence every positive-loop correction vanishes. The tree operation on a derivative of an odd partner is the matching derivative of its F-equation and extends to products as a graded derivation. [Graph and antifield count](../sources/all-loop/main.tex)
>
> Let $\delta$ be this Koszul operation with the one-instanton weight extracted and the fixed matching coefficients retained. The full differential is the weight times $\delta$. Closed formal series have each coefficient in $\ker\delta$. A boundary has zero constant coefficient and each higher coefficient lies in $\operatorname{im}\delta$. Localization kills precisely the classes represented by $\operatorname{im}\delta$ in the constant coefficient. This specifies all jet orders and all numbers of odd partners, including their polynomial relations. It uses the superpotential sector before adding any independent higher-derivative interaction.
>
> At six flavor coordinates use the matrix ordering in which the independent entries of $V$ are $V^{ij}$ with $1\le i<j\le6$, and $W=-\operatorname{Pf}V/\Lambda_L^3$. A concrete odd target is
>
> $$
> Q(\beta^{V^{12}}\beta^{V^{34}})
> =\frac{1}{\overline{\Lambda_L^3}}
> \left[
> \operatorname{Pf}\bar V_{\widehat1\widehat2}\,\beta^{V^{34}}
> -\operatorname{Pf}\bar V_{\widehat3\widehat4}\,\beta^{V^{12}}
> \right].
> $$
>
> The terms have distinct odd partners and nonzero signed minors. At zero magnetic instanton weight the differential vanishes. The bracket therefore gives a nonzero perturbative operator killed by the first power of that weight.
>
> In the sector stated, the source must have exactly $p$ odd partners, zero scalar letters, and zero derivatives. Indeed one variation replaces a single partner by two scalar letters, preserving total derivative order. Order the $p$ distinct pairs of flavor indices appearing in such a source. Varying it gives $p$ terms with alternating signs. Each has a signed minor identifying the one deleted index pair, and an exterior monomial identifying all $p-1$ others. Since the fifteen minors are linearly independent, any such component determines the original $p$-element subset. Thus the map is injective and these sources exhaust the sector. Summing the ranks over the fifteen exterior degrees gives $2^{15}-1=32767$ independent targets across the specified sectors. $\square$

### $SO(N_c)$: cancellation between instantons and condensates

Use the dynamical normalization of Intriligator–Seiberg.

Here $N_f$ counts individual vectors, and the dot denotes the invariant symmetric color form:

$$
M^{ij}=Q^i\mathbin{\cdot}Q^j,
\qquad
W_{\mathrm{mass}}=\frac m2M^{N_fN_f},
\qquad
W_{\mathrm{mag}}=\frac1{2\mu}M^{ij}q_i\mathbin{\cdot}q_j.
$$

The generic magnetic group is $SO(N_f-N_c+4)$, with generic-rank scale relation

$$
2^8\Lambda_{N_c,N_f}^{3(N_c-2)-N_f}
\widetilde\Lambda_{N_f-N_c+4,N_f}^{3(N_f-N_c+2)-N_f}
=(-1)^{N_f-N_c}\mu^{N_f}.
$$

For $N_f\le N_c-5$, $\epsilon_{N_c-N_f-2}$ is a condensate root of unity:

$$
W=\frac12(N_c-N_f-2)\epsilon_{N_c-N_f-2}
\left[\frac{16\Lambda_{N_c,N_f}^{3(N_c-2)-N_f}}{\det M}\right]^{1/(N_c-N_f-2)}.
$$

Two special branches retain additional information:

$$
W\big|_{N_f=N_c-4}
=\frac12(\epsilon_L+\epsilon_R)
\left(\frac{16\Lambda_{N_c,N_c-4}^{2N_c-2}}{\det M}\right)^{1/2},
\qquad \epsilon_L,\epsilon_R\in\{1,-1\},
$$

$$
W\big|_{N_f=N_c-3}
=4(1+\epsilon)\frac{\Lambda_{N_c,N_c-3}^{2N_c-3}}{\det M},
\qquad \epsilon\in\{1,-1\}.
$$

In the second equation, $4$ comes from broken-group instantons and $4\epsilon$ from the residual $SO(3)$ condensate.

They cancel on the $\epsilon=-1$ branch.

The source local superpotential on this branch contains a holomorphic function $f$:

$$
W=\frac1{2\mu}
f\left(\frac{(\det M)(M^{ij}q_iq_j)}{\Lambda_{N_c,N_c-3}^{2N_c-2}}\right)
M^{ij}q_iq_j,
\qquad f(0)=1.
$$

Here $q_i$ denotes massless composites.

The full Taylor coefficients of $f$ remain unspecified in the source.

One mass flow gives the two roots of $q_{N_f}^2=-\mu m$, corresponding to the opposite-sign branches at $N_f=N_c-4$.

Adding another mass gives the F-equation $m/2\ne0$ on the branch described by mesons, excluding that branch.

For electric $N_f=N_c-1$, magnetic $SO(3)$ requires

$$
W_{\mathrm{mag}}
=\frac1{2\mu}M^{ij}q_i\mathbin{\cdot}q_j
-\frac{\det M}{64\Lambda_{N_c,N_c-1}^{2N_c-5}}.
$$

> **Proof: the $1/64$ coefficient.**
>
> Start from the two equal-scale factors $s=L,R$ of magnetic $SO(4)$:
>
> $$
> 2^8\widetilde\Lambda_{s,N_c}^{6-N_c}\Lambda_{N_c,N_c}^{2N_c-6}=\mu^{N_c},
> \qquad q_{N_c}\mathbin{\cdot}q_{N_c}=-\mu m.
> $$
>
> The determinant of the light masses gives
>
> $$
> \widetilde\Lambda_{s,1}^{5}
> =\frac{\mu\det\widehat M}{2^8\Lambda_{N_c,N_c}^{2N_c-6}}.
> $$
>
> The two broken-factor instantons contribute
>
> $$
> W_{\mathrm{inst}}
> =\frac{2(\widetilde\Lambda_{L,1}^5+\widetilde\Lambda_{R,1}^5)}{-\mu m}
> =-\frac{\det\widehat M}{64m\Lambda_{N_c,N_c}^{2N_c-6}}
> =-\frac{\det\widehat M}{64\Lambda_{N_c,N_c-1}^{2N_c-5}}.
> $$
>
> After the full-rank magnetic quarks become massive, the source condensate is
>
> $$
> W_{\mathrm{cond}}=\epsilon\frac{\det M}{64\Lambda_{N_c,N_c-1}^{2N_c-5}},
> \qquad
> W_{\mathrm{total}}=(\epsilon-1)\frac{\det M}{64\Lambda_{N_c,N_c-1}^{2N_c-5}}.
> $$
>
> Cancellation at $\epsilon=1$ restores the electric flat branch.
>
> $\square$

At $N_f=N_c-2$, the theory is in a Coulomb phase, with source curve

$$
y^2=x^3+x^2(-U+8\Lambda_{N_c,N_c-2}^{2N_c-4})
+16\Lambda_{N_c,N_c-2}^{4N_c-8}x,
\qquad U=\det M
$$

The singularities at $U=0$ and $U=16\Lambda_{N_c,N_c-2}^{2N_c-4}$ are massless-state loci.

At low rank, $SO(4)$ has two scales, $SO(3)$ has instanton exponent $6-2N_f$, and $SO(2)$ is an Abelian endpoint.

Each uses its own source formulas.

[Intriligator–Seiberg](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9503179)

Global-form dualities also distinguish line spectra:

The line spectrum specifies which electric Wilson lines and magnetic flux lines are genuine operators of the theory.

Gauge groups with the same local Lie algebra can admit different sets of such line charges.

The subscripts in the following dualities retain this additional global information.

$$
Spin(N_c)\longleftrightarrow SO(N_f-N_c+4)_-,
\qquad
SO(N_c)_+\longleftrightarrow SO(N_f-N_c+4)_+,
\qquad
SO(N_c)_-\longleftrightarrow Spin(N_f-N_c+4).
$$

The signs $\pm$ denote the discrete theta/line-spectrum choices.

These source relations apply at ranks greater than four.

[Aharony–Seiberg–Tachikawa](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0318)
