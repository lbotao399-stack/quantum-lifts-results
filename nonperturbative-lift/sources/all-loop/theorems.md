# Perturbative inputs: differential and product

The permanent labels below are those of the [upstream manuscript](main.tex), preserved at commit `3ef33773984a525d2520a4f1ffaf60f3b29ee04f`. Both statements concern its perturbative local-operator construction.

<a id="b-00465"></a>

**Quantum master equation.** At `b-00465` (line 2756), assume the conjugate superpotential $\bar W$ is gauge invariant and the symmetric cubic trace of the matter representation $R$ vanishes: $\operatorname{Tr}_R(t_A\{t_B,t_C\})=0$ for gauge generators $t_A,t_B,t_C$. For the interaction in (B.10), each coefficient of its master equation vanishes. Lemma `b-00452` then gives $\mathbf Q^2=0$. With no matter the matter trace is zero. Neither this theorem nor its pure-gauge specialization constructs an instanton term.

<a id="b-00513"></a>

**Product on cohomology.** At `b-00513` (line 2893), assume the interaction satisfies the preceding quantum master equation. For closed inputs the quantum product induces an associative, graded-commutative, unital product on $H(\mathbf Q)$ (the quotient of closed operators by exact ones). Associativity is asserted on classes; the source gives a homotopy on representatives. Multiplication by the identity is strict. The successive pages carry the ordinary product of leading representatives, and each induced differential is a derivation. The application to the nilpotency index is in [§4](../../research/04-complex-and-products.md#np-5c85ee5e8a2c3293-1); the cochain homotopy is in [§9](../../research/09-secondary-pairing.md#np-b082a68ee6a1c259-1).
