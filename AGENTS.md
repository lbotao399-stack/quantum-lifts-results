# Research programme

For new quantum-lift research, begin with the versioned programme in
`research-roadmap/README.md`, its current `SCORECARD.md`, and the applicable
completion test in `MILESTONES.md`. Select one principal milestone and record
its concrete next calculation. The latest user instructions override the
roadmap; better methods and revised scientific questions are welcome.

Follow `research-roadmap/AGENTS.md` when updating progress or accepting a
milestone. Preserve exact evidence, complete native spaces and homogeneous
correction freedom. Keep formal cohomology, physical operators, instanton
maps and chaos observables distinct. Existing more specific instructions in
subdirectories continue to apply.

Respect the user's quarantine of immature highly innovative work. A public
roadmap audit does not authorize reading, delegating or publishing isolated
research. This roadmap release contains only established public evidence
and prospective research goals.
